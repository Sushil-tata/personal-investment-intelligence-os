# Owner Review Queue Definition (Wave 2A.3)

## Queue Item Schema
- item_type: THESIS | CLAIM | EVIDENCE | INTERPRETATION | PROVENANCE
- reference_id: deterministic entity identifier
- reason_code:
  - INCOMPLETE_PROVENANCE
  - UNMATCHED_EVIDENCE
  - THESIS_VERSION_BINDING_FAILURE
  - DUPLICATE_CANDIDATE
- details: object with machine-parsable context

## Required Workflow
- Queue items are generated by diagnostics and backfill dry-runs.
- Apply mode does not suppress queue generation.
- Resolution actions are external/manual and must append provenance.

## Guardrails
- Queue is read-only for existing historical records until human review.
- Conflict pairs (SUPPORTS and CONTRADICTS) are preserved as-is.
